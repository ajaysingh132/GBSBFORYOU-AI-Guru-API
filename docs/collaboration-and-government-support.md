# Expert, Collaborator and Government Support Plan

**Project:** GBSBFORYOU Native Language Model v0.1  
**Owner:** GBSBFORYOU, Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh  
**Status:** Seeking independent review and eligible partners; no partnership or grant is yet confirmed.

## 1. What we are asking for

We are seeking specific, verifiable contributions—not general endorsements alone.

### Technical and academic support
- Review of the proposed small-model-from-scratch scope, architecture, tokenizer, and training plan.
- Hindi and Indian-language corpus discovery, annotation advice, and license/provenance review.
- Independent baseline and held-out evaluation design.
- A mentor or academic collaborator who can review training feasibility and compute requirements.

### In-kind support
- Access to an academic lab or approved GPU compute.
- Student/research collaboration under a documented scope.
- Storage, hardware, cloud credits, or data preparation support.
- Mentoring, incubation, and help with grant applications.

### Financial support
- Prototype funding for a narrow, measurable pilot.
- A later-stage budget only after a reproducible training run and independent evaluation.
- Sponsorship must be documented in writing and must not be described as secured before approval.

## 2. Government and institutional pathways to investigate

### A. IndiaAI Compute Portal

Official portal: https://compute.indiaai.gov.in/

The portal offers access to AI compute services at subsidized/affordable rates for eligible users. It requires registration, verification, a project proposal, and a compute request. The portal publishes category-specific eligibility requirements; these may include academic/research credentials, startup recognition and experience, or an institutional authorization letter. **GBSBFORYOU must not assume eligibility as an individual project without checking the current criteria.**

Recommended action:
1. Prepare the model architecture, dataset plan, requested GPU type/hours, expected duration, and bill of materials.
2. Identify whether the applicant should be an eligible academic institution, researcher, startup/MSME, or another accepted category.
3. Seek a qualified university/incubator partner if the project itself does not satisfy a category.
4. Submit only after compute estimates and documentation have been reviewed.

### B. DST NIDHI-PRAYAS 2.0

Official programme: https://nidhi.dst.gov.in/prayas2-0/  
Official guidelines: https://nidhi.dst.gov.in/document/nidhi-prayas-2-0-program-guidelines/

The programme page describes prototype support through PRAYAS Centres and Advance PRAYAS Centres, with published ceilings of up to ₹20 lakh and ₹40 lakh respectively. These are programme ceilings, **not a promise of funding to this project**. Applications are routed through eligible centres/incubators and must meet current guidelines and centre-specific calls.

Recommended action:
1. Narrow the application to a prototype deliverable: licensed Hindi dataset inventory, reproducible data pipeline/tokenizer, a small training run, and independent evaluation.
2. Contact an eligible PRAYAS Centre/Advance PRAYAS Centre and request a fit/eligibility discussion.
3. Ask the centre which costs are eligible; do not assume the full four-person annual payroll is eligible.
4. Use the current guidelines and active call rather than relying on a summary or old application window.

### C. University, research lab, or technology business incubator

Approach institutions for a technical mentor, GPU access, evaluation partnership, and support in choosing a funding route. Any institution must be named as a partner only after it agrees in writing. Potential partner categories include universities with NLP/AI groups, engineering colleges, language departments, public research institutions, and recognized technology incubators.

### D. Other support

- Startup India / DPIIT recognition may be relevant if a legally eligible entity is established and the project meets applicable criteria: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/
- The official Startup India scheme playbook can help identify incubator and funding routes: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/dam/startupindia/homebanners/Startup-Schemes-Playbook-June-2026.pdf
- Government and academic data initiatives may offer datasets or partnerships, but each dataset's license and allowed uses must be checked separately.

## 3. What to send to a potential partner

Share the public proposal and budget, then ask for one clearly defined response:
- Can you review the technical feasibility and compute estimate?
- Can your institution mentor or host a small reproducible pilot?
- Which current grant or incubator route fits this stage?
- Can you provide a written estimate for GPU hours and storage?
- Can you independently review the Hindi evaluation plan?

Avoid asking a partner to endorse an untested claim that the project already has a trained model.

## 4. Suggested 30-day outreach sequence

| Week | Action | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Publish proposal, budget, team/workflow docs, and collaboration issue | Public links and commit records |
| 1–2 | Invite independent NLP, Hindi-language, and data-license reviews | Issue comments or written review |
| 2 | Prepare a compute bill of materials and GPU-hour estimate | Reviewed compute worksheet |
| 2–3 | Contact eligible incubators/PRAYAS Centres and academic labs | Contact log, eligibility feedback |
| 3 | Confirm applicant category and allowable costs | Written guidance or published call |
| 4 | Revise proposal and submit only to a matching active call | Submitted version and acknowledgement |

## 5. Transparency and safeguards

- No grant, institutional partnership, compute allocation, or sponsorship will be claimed until documented.
- Public GitHub issues must not contain personal data, credentials, private correspondence, or restricted datasets.
- Contributions and conflicts of interest should be recorded.
- Code, datasets, tokenizer, and model weights may have different licenses; each must be reviewed separately.
- A public repository is a collaboration mechanism, not grant approval or a guarantee of government support.
- The first-stage success criterion is a documented, reproducible feasibility result—not a claim of a production-ready foundation model.
